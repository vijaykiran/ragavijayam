How to read: each table = one āvartana of Rūpaka = 6 beats: 1 clap, 2 wave  ‖  3 clap, 4–6 finger counts. Each of your teacher’s | bars = one table.
Speed: one swara in a cell = one beat. Two swaras in a cell = half a beat each (her underlined groups). , = hold / rest for that beat or half-beat.
Octaves: plain = middle · red, dot above (ṡ ṙ ġ ṁ) = tāra · blue, dot below (ḍ ṇ) = mandra.
At Sa = C: s=C, r=D, g=E♭, m=F, p=G, d=A, n=B♭, ṡ=C′. Mandra ḍ = A, ṇ = B♭ below Sa.
