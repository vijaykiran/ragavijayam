Sharanu Sharanu Ganapati

Raga Hamsadhwani (S R2 G3 P N3) · Adi tala, tishra gati (8 beats × 3 pulses = 24 pulses per avartana) · Sruti: Sa = A · notation corrected against the teacher's recording
How to read: each table = one avartana (tala cycle). Each column = one beat holding 3 pulses (ta-ki-ta). Beats 1, 5, 7 are claps; 2–4 finger counts; 6, 8 waves. Thick borders mark laghu | drutam | drutam.
Swaras: plain = middle octave · red with dot (ṡ ṙ ġ) = upper (tara) octave · blue (ṇ) = lower (mandra) grace · , = hold previous note one pulse · joined letters (ṡnp, ṡn) = fast ornamental cluster on one pulse · underlined pair (ṙp) = two swaras sharing one pulse (double speed).
Alignment note: where a syllable stretches over holds, placement within the beat is approximate — the teacher's recording is the final authority on phrasing and gamakas.
