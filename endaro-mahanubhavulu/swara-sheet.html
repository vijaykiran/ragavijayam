Endaro Mahanubhavulu

Tyagaraja · Raga Sri (arohana S R2 M1 P N2 Ṡ · avarohana Ṡ N2 P M1 R2 G2 R2 S — 22nd melakarta janya) · Adi tala · Telugu · Pancharatna kriti no. 5 · Sruti: Sa = C
How to read: each boxed line = one avartana of Adi tala. The single bar | marks the laghu → drutam boundary (clap + 3 finger counts, then clap–wave, clap–wave); || closes the avartana.
Swaras: capital letters sit on the main counts · lowercase clusters (Rgr, sn, pmr…) are quick connected notes sharing a count · ; = rest/hold of two counts · , = hold one count.
Octaves: the printed paatham does not mark mandra/tara dots — pencil them in from class. Alignment of syllables under swaras is approximate in this long kriti; the teacher's rendition is the final authority.
